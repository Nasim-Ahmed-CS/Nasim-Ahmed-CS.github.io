I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Kennesaw State University and a Graduate Research Assistant at the Human-Centered Immersive eXperience Lab (HiX Lab).

My research focuses on building immersive systems that are safer, more adaptive, and more privacy-aware. I work across XR safety, cognitive and behavioral modeling, cybersickness, engagement, human-centered AI, and privacy/security for behavioral sensing.

Graduate Research Assistant on DARPA Intrinsic Cognitive Security research (Award HR00112420366).
