About

Research at the intersection of people and immersive computing.

About me

My work focuses on understanding and improving how people experience XR systems, with particular emphasis on safety, cognitive state modeling, personalization, trustworthy sensing, and privacy-preserving interaction. I am especially interested in methods that remain practical under real-world sensing and deployment constraints.

Current and selected projects

From the original Google Site, reorganized into a research-focused portfolio.

VeriPro

Verified probabilistic cognitive reasoning for tactical mixed-reality systems. My work focuses on cognitive modeling and cybersickness modeling within this DARPA-funded collaborative project.

DARPACognitive ModelingCybersickness

SafeAR

Research on safer AR walking and environmental hazard awareness using Meta Quest 3, progressing from static environment mapping toward dynamic safety-aware interaction.

Meta Quest 3AR SafetySpatial Computing

Towards Safer Mixed Reality

On-device hazard detection with auditory warnings for uncontrolled indoor settings, designed to alert users to spatial risk while maintaining immersion.

Anu — Digital Anubis

An immersive remembrance experience combining VR storytelling, AI-mediated dialogue, memory curation, and multimodal interaction, with future concepts for neuro-haptic extensions.
