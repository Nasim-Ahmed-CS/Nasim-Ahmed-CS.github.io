Research

Research themes

My research asks how immersive systems can infer user state responsibly, anticipate risk, personalize support, and remain practical under real sensing and deployment constraints.

01

Cognitive & behavioral modeling

I develop models for continuous cognitive load, engagement, user performance, and uncertainty-aware prediction across changing XR contexts. A recurring goal is to move beyond static labels toward temporally meaningful, user-sensitive estimates.

Cognitive LoadEngagementUncertaintyMultimodal Learning
02

XR safety & cybersickness

I study cybersickness forecasting, probabilistic verification, personalized susceptibility modeling, and hazard-aware MR systems. This includes both predictive modeling from multimodal signals and system-level interventions that help users before risk becomes critical.

CybersicknessSafetyBayesian NetworksFew-Shot Learning
03

Privacy & security for immersive systems

XR sensors capture behavioral signals that can expose sensitive attributes. My work explores targeted feature obfuscation, progressive adversarial privacy, and privacy–utility tradeoffs that reduce demographic leakage while preserving task-relevant information.

PrivacySecurityEye TrackingBehavioral Biometrics
04

Sponsored cognitive-security research

As a Graduate Research Assistant in HiX Lab, I contribute to DARPA-funded research on intrinsic cognitive security, including engagement, cognitive state modeling, immersive-system safety, and privacy. Award: HR00112420366.
